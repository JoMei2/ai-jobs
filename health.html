How to apply in 4 steps

  1. Sign up to two or three platforms. Work comes and goes on each one, so don't rely on a single site.
  2. Write your profile around what you can check. For example: "I can tell when medication advice is unsafe for older patients." Specific beats general.
  3. Do the AI interview. Treat it like a normal interview. Give real examples from your work and think out loud.
  4. Take the test. If it covers things outside your profession, tell the platform. They can often give you a version that fits.

Before you apply

  • Check the country list. Many of the best-paid roles are US only.
  • Advertised pay is usually the top of the range.
  • Work can be busy for a few weeks and then go quiet.
  • You should never pay to join. If a site asks for money, walk away.
